What Dubai competitors do

  • Most local companies: busy red and yellow graphics, stock bug pictures, a "shocked woman" stock photo, emoji captions and "Studio AED 99" offers. They all look the same.
  • Justlife (biggest budget): vertical videos made with Dubai creators (expat mums at home). Their longest-running pest ads say "safe for my little ones and pets" and "Dubai Municipality approved".
  • Rentokil: no active Meta ads in the UAE.
Our chance: in this market, a premium, calm, real-looking ad stands out immediately. We aim at villa owners, tower residents and hotels, not the cheapest customer.

Rules for every ad

  • Vertical first (9:16), message in the first 2 seconds.
  • Text on screen. Most people watch with the sound off.
  • One pest per ad. WhatsApp button as the call to action.
  • Real photos and videos always beat AI pictures. AI only fills in until we have real ones.
  • Never an AI person pretending to be a customer. That is a fake review.

For the teamHow to Film a Job Video

Five to seven short clips of 3 seconds each. No talking needed. Claude edits them into the ad.

Phone settings

  • Hold the phone upright (vertical).
  • Main camera, 1080p or 4K, 30 fps.
  • Wipe the lens first.
  • Lock focus: tap and hold on the subject.

How to film

  • Each clip 3 to 5 seconds, phone steady (lean on a wall).
  • Move slowly, no fast turns.
  • Daylight or all lights on. No backlight from a window behind the subject.
  • Film the same spot before and after, from the same place.

Look professional

  • Full uniform, black gloves, clean kit.
  • Equipment labels turned away.
  • Tidy the area around the shot.

Privacy

  • Ask the customer first: "Can we film the work for our page, without your face or flat number?"
  • No faces, flat numbers, car plates, family photos or documents.
Never film: customers or their children, the inside of rooms they did not agree to, or chemicals being sprayed into the air near people.

Send to Claude

  • All clips in order (WhatsApp as "document" so quality stays high)
  • Area and service, for example "JLT, pigeon netting, 2 bedroom balcony"
  • Customer said yes to filming

E. Customer video (only with written permission)

Ask a happy customer to film 15 seconds on their phone, in their own words. Give them these prompts, not a script:

  • What was the problem? ("Pigeons every morning on the balcony")
  • What did you like? ("Clean, quick, safe for my cat")
  • Would you recommend us?
Get a written yes on WhatsApp ("You can use my video in your ads") and save it. Never pay for a positive review. Paying a creator for a clearly labelled partnership ad is fine.

StartStarting From Zero Followers

Our Facebook and Instagram pages are brand new. That is fine for ads, but not for trust.

Good news

Ads do not need followers

Meta shows our ads to anyone in Dubai we choose (area, age, interests). A page with 0 followers can get WhatsApp chats from day one.
The catch

People check the profile first

Before they message, many tap the page name. An empty page looks like a fake or brand-new company, and they leave.

Step 1 · Set up the profiles (before any ad)

  • Instagram: professional (business) account, name "Prime Pest Control Dubai", category Pest Control Service
  • Bio: "Pest and bird control across Dubai · Dubai Municipality approved · Open 24/7 · WhatsApp 055 304 5256"
  • WhatsApp button on Instagram and Facebook, website link with tracking
  • Facebook page: same name, logo, cover, hours 24/7, services list, service area Dubai
  • Connect Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp in Meta Business Suite (needed for Click to WhatsApp ads)
  • Instagram highlights: Services · Before and After · Reviews · Areas · FAQ

Step 2 · Foundation grid: 9 posts in the first week

So the page looks established on day one. Post them over 4 to 7 days, not all in one minute.

Step 3 · Then

  • 3 posts a week plus Stories, Google Business post every week.
  • Start Click to WhatsApp ads once the 9 posts are up. Small budget, 3 to 4 creatives, keep the winners.
  • Ask every happy customer for a Google review and to follow the page. Share each post on your WhatsApp status.
