# Google review messages

Reviews are the strongest thing we can add for Google Maps and local search right now (the profile has 1).
Send the message the same day the job is finished, while the customer is happy.

## Review link

Use this link in every message (it opens the review box on the Google Business Profile directly):

**https://g.page/r/CQtAAXyhoqatEBM/review**

## 1. After the job (WhatsApp)

Replace [Name], [service] and [area]. Mentioning the service and area is good for the customer to write about,
but never tell them what to say.

> Hi [Name], thank you for choosing Prime Pest Control for the [service] in [area]. We hope you are happy with the work.
> If you have a minute, a Google review would help us a lot and helps other families in Dubai find us: https://g.page/r/CQtAAXyhoqatEBM/review
> You can write in any language, and photos of the work are very welcome. Thank you!

## 3. If a customer asks you to write the review for them

Do not write it, type it or post it for them. Google's rules say a review must be the customer's own words
about their own experience, posted from their own account; reviews written by the business can be removed
and can get the profile suspended. You can make it easy instead:

- Tell them it can be **one short sentence** in **any language** (Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Malayalam, anything):
  Google translates it for other readers. Stars and photos alone are fine too.
- They can **speak instead of type**: in the review box, tap the microphone on the phone keyboard and say it.
- If they are unsure what to say, ask them these questions and let them answer in their own words:
  1. What was the problem? (for example pigeons on the roof)
  2. What did we do? (for example spikes on the villa)
  3. How is it now?
  4. Would you recommend us?
- They should post it **from their own phone and Google account**, not from your phone, and not while you
  are standing next to them on your WiFi.
- Their own photos of the work are great: they make the review more trustworthy.

## 4. Rules (Google policy)

- Ask every customer, not only the happy ones you pick, and never ask for "5 stars only".
- No discounts, gifts or payment for reviews.
- No reviews from staff, family or friends who were not real customers.
- Never write a review for someone else, and never post several from one phone.
