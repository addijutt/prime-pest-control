# Pest and Bird Image Prompts

Prompts for the 62 pest, bird and method images the site still needs (list and pages: `docs/pest-image-list.md`).
Each prompt is written to match the images already on the site, so new tiles sit next to old ones without
looking different.

## How the Existing Images Look (match this)

- A **real-looking photo**, not an illustration, cartoon or 3D render.
- **One subject, cut out**, on a plain white or transparent background, centred, with nothing else in the frame.
- Seen from a **three-quarter top view** (slightly above and to the side), so the shape is easy to recognise.
- **Soft, even studio light**, no harsh shadows. A very light shadow under the subject is fine.
- The subject fills about **75% of the square**, with even space on every side.
- **No text, labels, arrows, logos, watermarks, hands or people.**

## How to Generate

1. Use a good photo-realistic image model (for example Midjourney, ChatGPT / DALL-E, Gemini / Imagen, Ideogram
   or Firefly).
2. Square image, **1024 × 1024 px or larger**, PNG.
3. Paste the **style block** below, then the **subject line** of the image you want, in one prompt.
4. Generate 3 or 4 versions and pick the most accurate one (check the list under "Before You Save").
5. Save the file with its **key** as the name, for example `camel-spider.png`, in one folder.

Send me the folder. I will remove the white background, centre and size each image, and save the 3 files the
site uses (`<key>.webp` 300 × 300, `<key>-d.webp` 150 × 150, `<key>-m.webp` 100 × 100, WebP with
transparency), then switch the tiles over from their stand-in images.

### Style Block (paste first, every time)

> Photorealistic macro product photograph of a single subject isolated on a pure white seamless background
> (#FFFFFF), centred, three-quarter top view, subject filling about 75% of a square frame with even white space
> around it, soft diffused studio lighting, very subtle natural contact shadow, sharp focus across the whole
> subject, true-to-life colours, high detail, scientifically accurate anatomy. No text, no labels, no logo, no
> watermark, no hands, no people, no props, no background scenery. Square 1:1.

### Negative Prompt (for tools that have a separate field)

> text, letters, watermark, logo, label, cartoon, illustration, 3D render, painting, hands, fingers, people,
> busy background, scenery, blur, extra legs, missing legs, extra wings, deformed, duplicate subject, cropped
> subject, frame, border

### Before You Save

- **Legs:** insects 6, spiders 8, adult ticks 8, tick larvae 6. AI often gets this wrong.
- **Antennae, wings and body segments** look like the real species (compare with a Google image search).
- The whole subject is inside the frame, nothing cut off.
- Background is plain white with no texture or shadows at the edges.

## Real Photos Beat Generated Ones

For the methods and signs (bird spikes, netting, gel, droppings, nests, mud tubes, termite damage), a real
photo from one of your jobs is better than a generated one and helps with Google. Take it close up against a
plain wall where you can; I can cut it out the same way.
