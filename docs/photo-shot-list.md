# Photo shot list

Generated from the photo briefs in `src/config/serviceGuides.ts` and `src/config/communities.ts`.
In the preview (`npm run dev`) each slot shows a labelled placeholder; on the live site it shows an existing
team photo until the real one is added.

**How to add a photo:** save it as `public/assets/img/jobs/<page>-<slot>.webp` (for example
`cockroach-control-dubai-intro.webp`) and add `src: '/assets/img/jobs/cockroach-control-dubai-intro.webp'`
to that brief in the config file.

**Every photo:** real job photo (no stock), landscape 4:3, at least 1200 × 900 px, technician in Prime uniform where
possible, customers' faces and flat numbers not visible, good light. Export as WebP under 250 KB.
