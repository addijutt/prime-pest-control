## 6. Links from local sites (the strongest ranking help)

Ask, do not buy. Ideas, easiest first:
- **Companies you already work with** (building management, facility management, AC maintenance, cleaning,
  interior fit-out, real estate agents): ask for a line on their "partners" or "recommended suppliers" page
  with a link to the website.
- **Community groups and newsletters** (villa community committees, building owner associations): offer a
  short "how to keep pigeons off your balcony" or "summer cockroach tips" article with a link.
- **Happy commercial customers** (restaurants, offices): ask if they would mention you on their site or
  social pages.
- **Local news and "best of Dubai" lists**: reply to journalists or list writers when they ask for pest or
  bird control experts.

Avoid paid link packages and link farms: Google can penalise the site for them.
