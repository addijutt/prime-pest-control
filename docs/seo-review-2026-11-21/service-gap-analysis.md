**Status 21 Nov 2026:** done: Fumigation, Restaurant, Office and Fly control pages; Dubai South and Dubailand pages; Crow and Sparrow merged into Bird Control. Open: warranty terms (owner), Dubai Municipality free-service FAQ on the homepage, flying cockroach FAQ, bed bug inspection price, water tank cleaning (only with DM approval), pantry and fabric pests page.

How this was checked: Google autocomplete for the UAE (what people type into Google here), the site's own
Search Console searches, the service menus of the main Dubai pest control companies, and Dubai Municipality
pages. Sources at the end.

## B. Services that exist but are incomplete

| Page | What is missing | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Every page | **Warranty.** "pest control dubai with warranty" is a common search, and the site never says what happens if pests come back. | Owner to give the warranty terms (for example 30, 60 or 90 days per service); then add them to the price cards and FAQs. |
| Homepage / FAQ | **The free Dubai Municipality service.** People search "dubai municipality pest control free". The municipality treats flies, mosquitoes and rodents for free for residents; other pests need a DM-approved company. | Add an honest FAQ explaining this. It builds trust and catches these searches. |
| Cockroach page | "flying cockroach dubai" is searched. | Add an FAQ: the large flying ones are American cockroaches from drains. |
| Bird control | Methods were missing until today (spikes, wire, gel, solar mesh are now covered). | Owner to confirm which methods the team installs (especially bird gel and wire). |
| Bed bugs | Heat or steam treatment is a common question. | Add it only if the team uses steam or heat. |

## C. Services or claims that are wrong or not worth a page in Dubai

Already removed earlier: the US-style pages (critter control, carpenter bees, yellow jackets, house centipedes),
"roach" pages and the US species in the pest grids.

Still worth reviewing:

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| **"Results 100% Guaranteed"** and **"Eco-responsible"** (Why Choose Us, homepage description) | Strong claims with no terms behind them. Replace with the real warranty once the owner gives it. |
| **Spider, silverfish, crow and sparrow pages** | Real problems, but very few searches. Keep them (they cost nothing and rank quickly), but do not spend money promoting them. |
| **Crow control** | House crows are partly controlled by Dubai Municipality itself in public areas. Our page should stay about private property (bins, terraces, roofs), as it is now. |
| **Sharjah and Abu Dhabi** | "pest control sharjah" and "pest control abu dhabi" are searched a lot, but the business and its Google profile serve Dubai. Only add these if the team really works there. |

## D. Suggested order

1. Fumigation page (licence already held, high search demand, competitors rank for it).
2. Restaurant pest control and Office pest control pages (B2B contracts, recurring revenue).
3. Fly control page.
4. Warranty terms on every page (needs the owner).
5. Dubai Municipality free-service FAQ, flying cockroach FAQ, bed bug inspection price.
6. Dubai South and Dubailand community pages.
7. Water tank cleaning (only with the DM approval).
