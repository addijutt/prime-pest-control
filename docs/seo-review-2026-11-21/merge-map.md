# Page plan: Prime Pest Control (version 2, 21 Nov 2026)

**Status 21 Nov 2026:** the owner confirmed all Dubai Municipality approvals (Q0), including termite
control, bird control and fumigation. Sections A (merges) and C (new addresses) are built and tested on
the developer's machine, not yet live. Still needed from the owner: Q3 (extra services), Q4 (prices),
Q5 (main communities).

Based on: every Google search the site has appeared for (Search Console, 21 Aug – 19 Nov 2026,
77 searches), visits in GA4, the first real leads, and research on what people in Dubai search for and
what the top Dubai pest control sites offer (sources at the end).

## Summary

1. **Before anything else: licence scope (question 0).** Dubai Municipality's list says a general pest
   control licence does *not* cover termite control, bird control or fumigation. Those need their own
   approval. About a third of the site's non-brand Google impressions are on the bird pages.
2. **Merge 8 groups of competing pages** (section A). 44 → 23 service pages. Every search the removed
   pages had is listed and moved to the page that stays, so no searches are lost.
3. **Give every remaining page one main search** (section B), so pages stop competing with each other.
4. **Change 6 addresses from "near me" to "Dubai"** (section C).
5. **Add the pages people in Dubai search for that the site does not have** (section D): move-in pest
   control, lizards, community pages (starting with Al Barari, JLT, JVC, Al Barsha, Studio City).

## E. What every page that stays needs (so it can win, not just exist)

- **Starting price in AED and number of visits** (competitors publish them: bed bugs about AED 350–500
  for a studio/1BR with 2 visits, AED 500–1,100 for 2–3BR).
- **The Dubai problem:** where this pest shows up in Dubai (apartment vs villa, season, community).
- **The treatment:** steps, safety for children and pets, how long to stay out, follow-up visit, warranty.
- **Its own FAQ** (4–6 questions people really ask), real photos, and a WhatsApp button that says which
  service the customer is asking about.
